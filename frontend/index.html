Bookvane · Your personal reading home

Bookvane is a free, private place to discover books, keep your whole library together, and track what you’re reading at your own pace. No social feed: your library, ratings and notes stay yours.

What you can do

  • Discover books by subject and search a growing catalogue with covers and descriptions.
  • Save books to your shelves: want to read, reading, finished, and books you own.
  • Track your reading with start and finish dates.
  • Make your own book lists.
  • Bring your library over from Goodreads or StoryGraph.
