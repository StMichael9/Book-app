# You, as a home: the profile rework

Branch `ui/v3-profile-rework`, from `origin/v3` (after the body-font fix, #106). Follows the brief "NEXT: ui/v3-profile-rework" items 1–11 (minus the font commit, which shipped as #106), the premium standard and the direction contract. Local commits only; not pushed.

## What changed
- **Avatar:** one `Avatar` component in sizes 32 (header), 22 (You tab), 68 (hub) and 96 (public and the picker preview). A lucide line icon on a bookcloth colour, the glyph about 44% of the diameter (30 px at 68, 42 at 96, 14 at 32, 10 at 22; stroke 1.75, or 2 at the two small sizes), initials at 40%. The ink is #fffdf5 where that reaches 4.5:1, plain white on sage, and a dark ink (#1a251f) on mustard (`avatarInk`, covered by `tests/avatar-contrast.test.mjs`). No icon shows the initials, no colour shows green. A change crossfades in 200 ms. Fields are `avatar_icon` / `avatar_color` (T24b); mocked in the specs until the backend lands.
- **Header and nav:** the 32 px avatar links to /profile with the accessible name "Your profile" (it now shows on phones too). The You tab shows a 22 px avatar and is active only on /profile, /profile/* and /account, never /u/*.
- **Hub order:** identity row, bio, privacy pill, "Your {year}", "Your lists", then an Account section with "Account settings ›" and "Sign out".
- **Edit profile:** the link sits in the name column under "@username · reading since". It opens one sheet (bottom sheet on phones, dialog on desktop) with a live 96 px preview, Icon (4×3 radios), Colour (6 named swatches, 2 columns), name, username and bio, with the existing validation. Save and Cancel are pinned at the bottom; Esc cancels; focus returns to the Edit profile button.
- **Make public:** opens a confirm sheet with the same "Shown / Always private" lines as the one-time sheet, asking for a username first when none is set. Make private stays instant. A polite live region says "Your profile is now public." / "…private."
- **Year:** a spine shelf on a 3 px ink baseline: one spine per finished book in its month, month initials below (biggest month bold). Height (78–100% of the shelf) and colour come from a hash of (year, month, index), so they are stable and mean nothing. Spines are 5 px on phones and 12 px on desktop and never go below 4 px (phone) or 8 px (desktop); each has the jacket's lighter band 12% from the top. A month with more books than fit shows what fits plus a small "+N" above it (the screen-reader list still gives the full count); they rise 240 ms on first paint, staggered by 20 ms per month, once per page load. Genres are plain muted text ("Mostly Mystery (7) · Classics (5)"). An empty year shows the baseline and "Finish a book this year and it'll show up here."
- **Lists:** a card grid (two across, one when narrow or at large text). Artwork is up to four covers in 2:3 frames; a missing, loading or failed cover is a bookcloth spine block (colour from a hash of the book id), never a letter tile. The spine block behind a loading cover appears only after about 180 ms, and never for a cover the browser already holds. Each card shows the name, "N books" and Lock "private" / Globe "shared"; "+ New list" sits in the section header.
- **List page:** a 96 px artwork strip; rows use `BookCover` (the typographic jacket stands in for missing art). Edit mode gives each row 44 px Move up, Move down and Remove icon buttons on the same line. Remove collapses the row (200 ms) and shows "Removed {title}. Undo" for 6 s; Undo re-adds the book and restores its position (the row opens up again). A moved row slides 160 ms. "Done" is a filled green button. "Rename or delete" opens a sheet with name, description, Save and "Delete list" (the existing confirm).
- **Public pages:** the same layout without the pill, edit or Account section: a 96 px avatar, the year shelf (third person), list cards and the guest line. A 404 sets the tab title "Page not found · Bookvane".
- **Consistency:** one tertiary link style (green, underlined) for Make public, Make private, New list, Edit profile, Edit, Rename or delete, Account settings and Sign out. The `h2:focus{outline:none}` rule is gone (headings use the standard ring). Lora is used at three sizes through global tokens in `bookvane.css :root` (`--bv-fs-page`, `--bv-fs-section`, `--bv-fs-card`, plus `--bv-fs-helper`); helper text is 14 px, body 16 px. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Review round 2
- **Year shelf in dark and at large text:** the spine capacity was measured from the month columns, whose width depends on their own contents, so some captures collapsed to one spine per month. It is now computed from the shelf's own width, and a zero-width measure shows every spine. New test: spine counts per month in light, dark and 200% text.
- **Nav labels at 200%:** columns grow to the longest word and labels wrap only between words (`overflow-wrap: normal; word-break: keep-all`); the `+N` label is anchored above its column so it never overlaps spines.
- **Guest You tab:** signed-out visitors get the plain person icon, never an avatar (test added).
