# V3 minimalist refinement — visual comparison

Before: published manual-test build `426667e81eb4b2d0c2c8dd19515f6581a25fb1e9`. After: final application `915bee78c6b4def6ec7b62d86eda336cfb5018f3`. All screens use synthetic fixtures, not real readers or private accounts. Historical captures retain their original attribution. Each asset manifest records hashes and source commits.

Existing colours, typography and identity are preserved. The changes simplify editor placement, separate the current-reading desk from the collection, and reduce repetitive controls. These are design decisions and regression observations, not user-validated conversion gains.
