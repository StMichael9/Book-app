# November 14: the reading page, Direction D ("the one box")

Part 1 only (frontend). Wording is the brief's; nothing else was added.

- **The box (`ProgressBox`):** one "Page or %" input and Save, on Home's Reading now cards and at the top of `/my-books/{id}` (where a second input, "Add a thought", sits under it). `52` is a page; `35%` or `35 %` is a percent; `inputmode="decimal"`. The unit is whatever was typed. A page with no known total asks "How many pages in your copy?" once, inline, and saves the total with it. Errors sit at the field: "Type a page, like 52, or a percent, like 35%." and "Your copy has 360 pages." A saved value shows "Saved. Page 52, 14%." in a `role="status"` line and the input clears. A thought is appended to the reading's note.
- **Finished prompt:** at the last page or 100%, inline under the box: "Finished the book?" with "Yes, finished" (the finish call) and "Not yet". No dialog.
- **The page:** header (Back link, "•••" menu holding Want to read, I own it, Add to list and Pin to Up next), small cover, title, author, status chip, the fore-edge strip (decorative), the text line ("Page 52 of 360 · 14% · started 13 Nov 2026"; tapping the total edits it), the box, Pause and Finish book (never disabled by the box), "Your reading" journal (newest first, each line with a quiet Edit that opens it in place), and one fold, "About this book · Manage".
- **Removed from the page:** the big cover block, the My note section, the Why I saved this book disclosure (now a journal line), Update progress and its inline editor and the "Track by" select, the "Save or close the open editor" line, the duplicate history buttons, "Format, copy & dates", Next after this until the book is finished, and the Reading history disclosure (the journal replaces it).
- **Dates:** one format, "13 Nov 2026" (journal lines drop the year in the current year). No ISO date shows.
- **Part 2 (T55):** the save sends `on` only when `SEND_PROGRESS_DAY` (in `progressInput.js`) is true; it is false because the endpoint rejects unknown fields today. The journal shows one line per entry of `progress_days` ("14 Nov · page 14 → 52" or "20% → 35%") when the workspace or entry response carries it; a missing or empty list shows nothing.
